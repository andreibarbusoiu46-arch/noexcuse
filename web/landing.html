Questions, answered.

What counts as proof?

A photo taken with the camera in the app during the window that clearly shows the commitment: you at the rack in gym clothes, the open book on today’s page, the running track. You write what counts as proof for each commitment, and the AI explains every ruling in one sentence.

Can I use a photo I already have?

Not for the main proof. The camera in the app has no gallery button, and a capture has to be sent within three minutes, inside the window. The stricter Premium levels can additionally ask for a screenshot, for example a workout summary.

What happens when I miss?

You lose 100 points and your streak resets. The app then shows when your next chance is and helps you plan a comeback, such as a clearer definition of what counts as proof.

What if verification is delayed?

Your proof is saved with the time it arrived, and the target isn’t marked as missed while it waits. A delay on our side never costs you points.

Who can see my photos?

Only you. Proof photos are stored privately and are never shown to other members or partners.

Where can I use it?

Today NoExcuse runs on the web, in the browser on your phone or computer. An iPhone app comes next.
