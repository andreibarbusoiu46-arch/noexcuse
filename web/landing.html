Cross‑examination.

What counts as evidence?

A photo taken with the in‑app camera during the window that clearly shows the habit: you in running gear outside, the open book on page 40, the gym floor. The judge explains every ruling in one sentence.

Why can’t I upload a photo I already have?

Because then it could be from any day. NoExcuse only admits photos taken live, inside the window, so a verified habit means you actually did it.

What happens if I miss a day?

You lose 100 points and your streak resets. The app then hands you a comeback plan, so the next target is the one that counts.

Can I use it on Android or a computer?

NoExcuse is built for iPhone first. You can sign in and manage your habits on the web today; live evidence is designed for your phone’s camera.
