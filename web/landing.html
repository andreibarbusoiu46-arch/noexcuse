Questions, answered.

What counts as evidence?

A photo taken with the in‑app camera during the window that clearly shows the habit: you in gym clothes at the rack, the open book on today’s page, the running track. You can write what counts as proof for each habit, and the judge explains every ruling in one sentence.

Why can’t I upload a photo I already have?

Because then it could be from any day. NoExcuse only accepts photos taken live, inside the window, so a verified habit means you actually did it.

What happens when I miss?

You lose 100 points and your streak resets. The app then offers a comeback plan, such as an earlier reminder or a clearer definition of what counts as proof, so the next window goes better.

What if the verification is delayed?

Your evidence is saved with the time it arrived, and the target isn’t marked as missed while it waits for a ruling. A delay on our side never costs you points.

Can I use it on Android or a computer?

NoExcuse is built for iPhone first. You can sign in and manage your habits on the web today; live evidence is designed for your phone’s camera.
